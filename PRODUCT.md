# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Students exploring degree programs and nearby universities.

## Product Purpose
CourseNFound connects a 30-question interest assessment across nine domains with course recommendations, a searchable program library, comparisons, and university proximity information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve Firebase authentication, email verification, onboarding, profile management, saved assessment history, favorites, course comparisons, location fallbacks and theme support. Swipe Match and Day in a Life are coming-soon surfaces, not functioning assessments.

## Brand Commitments
The user confirmed keeping the CourseNFound name and supplied logo; the rest of the interface may be redesigned.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing React/Vite application, local degree and university catalogs, Firebase rules and test suites. Salaries and tuition are estimates; no outcome guarantees or fabricated usage statistics.

## Product Principles
Make course exploration understandable. Keep estimates explicit. Let guests explore before signing in. Protect saved information by account ownership.
